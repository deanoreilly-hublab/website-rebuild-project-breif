HubLab brings definitive AI to Australia's regulated work, with CERTAiNTY the first product live

Melbourne, [launch date]. HubLab, Australia's regulatory intelligence layer, today launched its rebuilt website and set out how it puts definitive AI into the regulated work of Australian enterprise and government.

Every regulated organisation runs on systems that manage processes well and stop at the hard part. The system tells you what arrived. It cannot tell you what it means. That gap is where non-compliance lives, and since January 2025 it carries personal exposure for directors.

HubLab closes that gap with definitive AI: AI that encodes the rule itself, gives the same answer every time, and traces every decision to the clause that made it. A person signs off every finding. The record stands up.

The engine is Labrynth, a regulatory intelligence engine built in the United States and proven in government there, exclusive to HubLab in Australia and a founding partner, not a vendor. HubLab is the layer on top: a local team that helps you find the real problem under the brief, builds the product for Australian rules, and owns the outcome. Customer success and operations run with Hubify (ASX: HFY), HubLab's strategic investor and operational partner, and HubLab's own forward-deployed engineers, in Australian hours.

The first product live is CERTAiNTY, the first insurance-backed payroll assurance in Australia. CERTAiNTY sits above an organisation's existing payroll system, reads the award or agreement for every shift, and certifies the pay run before it locks. In live production for a national facilities services group it has validated 80,000+ shifts across 4,000+ employees, with 120+ Modern Awards ingested. The same engine now lives in NDIS integrity and is built for planning and construction.

"The rules are written. Someone has to read them, every cycle," said Mark Dinnison, Chief Executive Officer of HubLab. "We built the layer that reads them the way a specialist would, and leaves a record a regulator, an auditor or a court can follow. One engine, many domains, and one accountable party on the ground in Australia."

"Australia has some of the most complex regulations in the common-law world, which makes it the right place to prove this," said Stuart Lacey, founder of Labrynth and board advisor to HubLab. "HubLab is the only door to Labrynth in Australia, and the work it has done in payroll shows what definitive AI looks like in production."

HubLab works with regulated organisations in a fixed sequence: a HubLab-run discovery in weeks, a working prototype you can touch, and a measured result beside the customer's production systems before anything further is committed.

HubLab's board is chaired by Charbel Nader, with Pat LaManna as director and Stuart Lacey as board advisor. HubLab is headquartered in Melbourne. To start a conversation, visit hublab.com.au.

Frequently asked questions

These are the answers we give in public. Each is complete on its own, so any one can be placed on a page as written.

What is HubLab?

HubLab is Australia's regulatory intelligence layer. We put definitive AI into the regulated work of Australian enterprise and government. One engine, many domains, one accountable party on the ground in Australia.

What problem do you solve?

Interpretation. The systems regulated organisations already run (payroll, case management, permitting, core banking) manage processes well and stop at the hard part. The system tells you what arrived. It cannot tell you what it means. That gap is where non-compliance lives.

What is definitive AI, and how is it different from the AI I already have?

Probabilistic AI predicts the likely answer. It is useful for drafting and hard to defend to a regulator, an auditor or a court. Definitive AI encodes the rule itself: awards, agreements, mandates, planning codes. Same input, same answer, every time, with the reasoning shown and every decision traced to the clause that made it. A person signs off every finding.

Who is it for?

Australian enterprise and government with regulated work to get right: payroll and awards, NDIS and aged care, planning and construction, financial services and government services.

Who are HubLab, Labrynth and Hubify, and who do I deal with?

You deal with HubLab.

Labrynth is the engine, a regulatory intelligence engine built in the United States and proven in government there, exclusive to HubLab in Australia and a founding partner, not a vendor.

HubLab is the layer: the local experts and execution team who help you find the real problem, build for Australian rules and own the outcome.

Hubify (ASX: HFY) is our strategic investor and operational partner. Local team, global engine.

How does an engagement work?

We run discovery with you in weeks, inside your business, your ways of working and your compliance obligations. Then a working prototype you can touch, and a measured result beside your production systems before anything further is committed.

Discovery, build, licence, each priced on the evidence of the stage before.

What is CERTAiNTY?

CERTAiNTY is the first insurance-backed payroll assurance in Australia. It sits above your existing payroll system, reads the award or agreement for every shift, and certifies the pay run before it locks. Each run leaves an immutable record: the workforce data, the awards applied, the logic and the outcome. Checked. Proven. Protected.

Is CERTAiNTY proven?

Yes. In live production for a national facilities services group, CERTAiNTY has validated 80,000+ shifts across 4,000+ employees, with 120+ Modern Awards ingested.

What else is the engine doing?

Payroll assurance is live. NDIS integrity is live. Planning and construction is in build. In Lancaster, California, the same engine took planning permits from 188 days to 8.

Do I have to replace my systems?

No. HubLab sits above the systems you already run. No rip and replace.

Where is my data held, and who can see it?

Each customer runs in its own segregated environment, hosted in Australia. All data is stored onshore in Australia, always. Every finding is reviewed and signed off by a person before it is acted on, and the audit record is yours. Detail on security and data handling is on the About us page.

Can you name your customers?

Not without their written approval. We describe them by sector: a national facilities services group, a state government.

How do I start?

Contact us through hublab.com.au. The first conversation is about the problem, not a proposal.
