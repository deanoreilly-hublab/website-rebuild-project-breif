The gap

Interpretation is the gap. Since January 2025, the exposure is personal.

The systems you already run manage process well and stop at the hard part. Payroll, case management, permitting, core banking: each tells you what arrived. None tells you what it means. Deliberate underpayment is now a criminal offence, with personal exposure for directors, and the question a regulator asks is not whether you had a system. It is whether you can show the decision, the clause and the person who signed it.
